Education snapshot

  • 1,576 public schools, 2,083 government-subsidized and 1,337 private

  • 14,104,969 people in Rwanda in 2025 (NISR projection); learners are 34.2% of them
  • 1,991 schools with secondary level and 581 with TVET
  • 57:1 pupils per classroom in primary schools

Source: MINEDUC Education Statistical Yearbook 2024/2025.

About Manthedan · RwandaMap AI · SchoolMap AI Rwanda

What is RwandaMap AI?

RwandaMap AI is an AI-powered mapping platform by Manthedan. It makes information about Rwanda's institutions, services, schools and communities easier to discover, understand and access.

SchoolMap AI Rwanda is its first application. It combines school data, geographic visualization and AI-assisted analysis to show how schools are distributed and where access may need a closer look.
